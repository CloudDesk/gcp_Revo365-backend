TEQIT (hereinafter referred to as "LESSOR") and {{lessee.customerCompanyName}} (hereinafter referred to as "LESSEE") are hereinafter individually referred to as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations, and agreements set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS

For the purposes of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them below:

  • "Equipment" or "Device" means the laptops and any associated accessories (bags, chargers, peripherals) listed in Annexure I to this Agreement.
  • "Rental Period" means the duration commencing from the date of delivery of the Equipment to the Lessee and ending as specified in Clause 3 or upon earlier termination in accordance with this Agreement.
  • "Rental Charges" means the monthly rental fee payable by the Lessee to the Lessor as specified in Annexure I, exclusive of applicable taxes.
  • "Security Deposit" means the refundable deposit paid by the Lessee as security against damage, theft, loss, or breach of this Agreement.
  • "Damage" means any physical harm to the Equipment including but not limited to breakage, burns, scratches, cuts, water damage, liquid spills, screen damage, keyboard damage, or any other harm not constituting normal wear and tear.
  • "Theft / Loss" means the disappearance of, or inability to return, the Equipment due to theft, robbery, burglary, negligence, misplacement, or any other cause.
  • "Market Replacement Value" means the current retail price of an equivalent or identical device at the time of loss, theft, or damage as determined by the Lessor.
2. GRANT OF LEASE

The Lessor hereby leases to the Lessee, and the Lessee hereby takes on lease from the Lessor, the Equipment described in Annexure I to this Agreement, on the terms and conditions set forth herein. The Lessee acknowledges that the Equipment is the exclusive property of the Lessor and that this Agreement confers no right of ownership whatsoever upon the Lessee.

3. RENTAL PERIOD AND MINIMUM LOCK-IN
  • This Agreement shall be effective from the date of signing and shall remain in force for a minimum lock-in period of {{commercial.minimumLockInMonths}} months ("Minimum Term"), commencing from the date of delivery of the Equipment.
  • After the expiry of the Minimum Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew on a month-to-month basis unless either Party provides written notice of termination at least 30 (thirty) days in advance.
  • Early termination by the Lessee before completion of the Minimum Term shall result in forfeiture of the Security Deposit and the Lessee shall be liable to pay the balance rental amount for the remaining Minimum Term as liquidated damages.
  • Early termination by the Lessor for reasons other than breach by the Lessee shall result in the Lessor refunding the Security Deposit within 15 business days of Equipment return, with no further liability.

4. RENTAL CHARGES AND PAYMENT TERMS
  • The monthly Rental Charges shall be as specified in Annexure I. All charges are exclusive of GST and other applicable taxes, which shall be payable by the Lessee in addition.
  • Rental for the first month shall be calculated on a pro-rata basis from the date of delivery.
  • From the second month onwards, rental shall be paid in advance on or before the 5th (fifth) day of each calendar month.
  • All payments shall be made by NEFT / RTGS / UPI / account payee cheque in favour of TEQIT. Cash payments shall not be accepted.
  • In the event of delayed payment beyond the 5th of any month, a late payment penalty of 2% (two percent) per month on the outstanding amount shall be levied and shall be payable in addition to the due rental.
  • If payment remains outstanding for more than 15 (fifteen) days beyond the due date, the Lessor reserves the right to repossess the Equipment without further notice and without liability.
  • The Lessor reserves the right to revise the Rental Charges with 30 (thirty) days' prior written notice to the Lessee. Any revision shall apply from the next billing cycle after the notice period.
5. SECURITY DEPOSIT / Advance rental
  • The Lessee shall, prior to or at the time of delivery of the Equipment, pay a Security Deposit equivalent to {{defaultText commercial.securityDepositMonths "___"}} months' rental to the Lessor by account payee cheque or bank transfer.
  • The Security Deposit shall be held by the Lessor as security against damage, theft, loss, unpaid rentals, or any other breach by the Lessee under this Agreement.
  • The Security Deposit shall NOT be adjusted against any monthly rental.
  • Upon the satisfactory return of Equipment in good working condition, settlement of all outstanding dues, and completion of the Rental Period, the Security Deposit shall be refunded within 15 (fifteen) business days after the Lessor's inspection confirms no damage or loss.
  • If damage or loss is identified upon return, the cost of repair or replacement shall be deducted from the Security Deposit, and any shortfall shall be payable immediately by the Lessee.
6. LOGISTICS AND DELIVERY
  • Delivery of Equipment shall be made to the address specified by the Lessee. The Lessee shall ensure a responsible person is available to receive and sign the Delivery Acknowledgement (Annexure II) at the time of delivery.
  • All logistics costs, including transportation, packaging, and insurance in transit, for delivery and return of Equipment shall be borne by the Lessee at actuals, unless otherwise agreed in writing.
  • Risk of loss or damage to the Equipment passes to the Lessee upon delivery and remains with the Lessee until the Equipment is physically received back by and signed for by an authorised representative of the Lessor.

  • The Lessor warrants that the Equipment shall be delivered in good working order and functional condition.

8. LESSEE’S OBLIGATIONS – CARE AND USE OF EQUIPMENT

The Lessee hereby agrees to the following obligations regarding the use and care of the Equipment:

  • Use the Equipment only for lawful business purposes and in a careful and proper manner, consistent with how a prudent owner would use their own property.
  • Not use the Equipment for any illegal activities, including but not limited to piracy, hacking, data theft, or any activity in violation of applicable law.
  • Keep the Equipment in a secure location and take all reasonable precautions to prevent theft, damage, or loss.
  • Not allow any third party to use the Equipment without written permission from the Lessor. The Lessee shall be liable for any damage caused by third-party use, whether authorised or not.
  • Not open, dismantle, modify, attempt to repair, or tamper with the Equipment in any manner without prior written consent from the Lessor. Any such unauthorised interference shall void all maintenance obligations of the Lessor, and the Lessee shall be liable for all resulting damage.
  • Not remove, deface, or alter any identification marks, asset tags, serial number stickers, or other identification markings placed on the Equipment by the Lessor.
  • Not install unauthorised or pirated software on the Equipment. Lessee assumes full legal and financial responsibility for any software installed by them.
  • Not pledge, mortgage, sub-lease, assign, or otherwise encumber the Equipment or any interest therein.
  • Immediately notify the Lessor in writing in the event of any damage, malfunction, theft, or loss of Equipment.

9. LIABILITY FOR DAMAGE

The Lessee shall be fully liable for any physical damage to the Equipment occurring during the Rental Period, including but not limited to:

  • Accidental damage: drops, screen cracks, Screen Line, keyboard damage, broken hinges, damaged ports.
  • Liquid damage: water, beverages, or any liquid ingress into the Equipment.
  • Burn damage: heat-related damage from exposure to fire, sunlight, or other heat sources.
  • Vandalism or deliberate damage.
  • Damage caused by use of the Equipment with unauthorised or incompatible accessories or power adaptors.

Upon occurrence of damage:

  • The Lessee shall immediately notify the Lessor in writing with a detailed description and photographs of the damage.
  • The Lessor shall assess the damage and raise an invoice for repair or replacement at the Market Replacement Value.
  • The Lessee shall settle the invoice within 7 (seven) business days of its receipt.
  • Monthly Rental Charges shall continue to accrue until full payment of the damage invoice is received, even if the Equipment is non-functional.
  • The Lessor shall not be responsible for any loss of data, software, or applications on the Equipment. Reinstallation of OS or applications shall not waive the Lessee’s liability for damage.
10. LIABILITY FOR THEFT, LOSS, OR MYSTERIOUS DISAPPEARANCE

This clause is of paramount importance, and the Lessee acknowledges having read and understood it fully:

  • In the event of theft, robbery, burglary, or loss of any Equipment, the Lessee shall bear full financial responsibility and shall compensate the Lessor at the Market Replacement Value of the stolen or lost Equipment.
  • The Lessee shall report the theft or loss to the concerned police authorities and lodge a First Information Report (FIR) or Non-Cognizable Complaint (NC) immediately upon discovering the theft or loss and shall furnish a certified copy of the FIR / NC to the Lessor within 24 (twenty-four) hours of filing the same.
  • The filing of an FIR / NC does NOT absolve the Lessee of financial liability to the Lessor. The Lessee shall remain liable to pay the Market Replacement Value regardless of whether the culprit is found or the device is recovered.
  • In the event of a mysterious disappearance (i.e., the Equipment cannot be found and there is no evidence of theft), the same shall be treated as theft for the purposes of this Agreement, and the Lessee shall be fully liable.
  • The Lessee must provide a letter on company letterhead describing the circumstances of the theft or loss, duly signed by an authorised signatory of the Lessee company, within 3 (three) business days of the incident.

11. MAINTENANCE, SUPPORT, AND REPAIRS
  • The Lessor shall maintain the Equipment in good working condition during the Rental Period at its own cost, provided the Equipment has not been damaged by the Lessee.
  • In the event of a hardware malfunction or failure due to normal wear and tear, the Lessor shall attend to the issue within 3 (three) working days of receipt of written notification from the Lessee.
  • A replacement device may be provided at the Lessor’s discretion while the original device is under repair. Rental shall continue for the period of repair.
  • Repair or replacement of Equipment damaged by the Lessee shall be carried out only after the Lessee has paid the applicable damage invoice as specified in Clause 9.
  • The Lessor and its employees or authorised agents shall not be responsible for any loss of data, application licensing, or any other software-related loss arising out of maintenance activities, OS reinstallation, driver updates, or troubleshooting operations.
  • The Lessee shall provide reasonable access and cooperation to the Lessor’s representatives for the purpose of inspection, maintenance, repair, or recovery of Equipment.
12. OWNERSHIP AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
  • The Lessor shall at all times retain full ownership, title, and interest in and to the Equipment. No right or interest of ownership, whether express or implied, shall be transferred to the Lessee by virtue of this Agreement or the payment of rentals.
  • The Lessee shall have no right to sell, transfer, assign, pledge, mortgage, hypothecate, sub-let, or otherwise deal with the Equipment as if they were the owner.
  • Any attempt by the Lessee to assign or transfer the Equipment or any right there in without the Lessor’s written consent shall be void and shall constitute a material breach of this Agreement.
  • The Lessor’s name, logo, brand, and any proprietary software installed on the Equipment remain the sole intellectual property of the Lessor.

13. RETURN OF EQUIPMENT
  • Upon expiry or termination of this Agreement, the Lessee shall return all Equipment to the Lessor within 3 (three) business days, in the same condition as received (fair wear and tear excepted), along with all accessories (chargers, bags, peripherals) listed in Annexure I.
  • The Lessee shall ensure all personal and/or company data is backed up and removed from the Equipment prior to return. The Lessor shall bear no responsibility for data remaining on the Equipment at the time of return.
  • Upon receipt, the Lessor shall inspect the Equipment within 3 (three) business days. Any damage or missing accessories identified shall be documented and invoiced to the Lessee.
  • If the Lessee fails to return the Equipment within the prescribed period, the Lessor reserves the right to:
  • Continue to charge rental at the prevailing monthly rate on a per-day pro-rata basis until the Equipment is returned.
  • Treat the non-return as theft and initiate legal proceedings as appropriate.
  • Deduct the Market Replacement Value from the Security Deposit and claim the balance from the Lessee.
  • Failure to return Equipment shall NOT be treated as a purchase. No payment, however large, shall create a right of ownership in the Lessee.
14. BREACH, REPOSSESSION, AND TERMINATION

Any of the following events shall constitute a material breach of this Agreement:

  • Non-payment of Rental Charges for more than 15 (fifteen) days beyond the due date.
  • Theft, loss, or deliberate damage to the Equipment.
  • Unauthorised sub-letting, transferring, or pledging of the Equipment.
  • Relocation of Equipment without prior written consent.
  • Tampering or unauthorised modifications to the Equipment.
  • Insolvency, winding up, or bankruptcy of the Lessee.
  • Any other material breach of the terms of this Agreement.
